The conference in brief

Faith in Christ, made visible in daily life.

Across the sessions, speakers connected trust in Jesus Christ with covenant worship, spiritual resilience, compassion for others, and choices that reflect discipleship. These notes synthesize the official program summaries; use the linked talks for the full messages.

Turn to Jesus Christ

Christ is presented as Deliverer, Comforter, Light, and the One who lifts us. Remembering God's goodness and trusting His promises can sustain hope through affliction and uncertainty.

Let covenants shape the week

Temple worship, ordinances, the sacrament, prayer, scripture study, fasting, and tithing are not isolated duties; they can form a steadier, more resilient pattern of discipleship.

See people as God sees them

Messages on divine appointments, peacemaking, teaching, and Christlike love call me to replace quick judgment with attention, kindness, and useful service.

Choose courage, integrity, and wise use of time

Faith can move us to act on prophetic counsel, share testimony, pursue worthy aspirations, live with integrity, and spend our time on what brings lasting good.
