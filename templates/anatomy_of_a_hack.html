Findings

SOC Analyst Value

  • Rapid triage support using prioritized detection points.
  • Evidence-backed reporting suitable for tabletop and remediation review.

Security Recommendations

The write-up highlights critical improvements such as network segmentation, endpoint monitoring, and alert tuning to detect the same attack chain earlier in the lifecycle.
