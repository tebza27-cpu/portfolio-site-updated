Starting and Growing My Business | Chapter 11

Continually Improving My Business

Chapter 11 connects a clear vision with the daily practices that help a business become more consistent: listen for guidance, document the work, meet legal responsibilities, develop people, and measure progress.

5Areas for continual improvement
SMARTGoals linked to the vision

My Foundation

Communicate: petition and listen

The chapter begins by treating listening as part of prayer and as a practical business skill. Careful listening helps me understand people before I decide how to respond.

01
Concentrate

Give the speaker attention

Notice words and body language, avoid interrupting, and put away distractions.

02
Appreciate

Show respect

Look at the person, acknowledge what they share, and thank them for speaking.

03
Review

Reflect back what I heard

Summarize the main concern in my own words so the other person can correct or clarify it.

04
Ask

Check my understanding

Ask whether I understood correctly, then wait and listen to the answer.

Part 2 | Vision

A picture of the business I intend to build.

A vision describes a desired future state and guides where I put my effort. My vision is a dependable, professional IT service with clear offers, steady customer relationships, and systems that let me grow without sacrificing service quality. The work is to keep returning to that direction and take practical steps toward it.

Part 3 | Operations

Make routine work repeatable.

The book recommends documenting standard workflows so quality does not depend on one person remembering every step. Manuals can support consistency, training, coverage when someone is away, and regular improvement.

DOCUMENT

Write down the standard

Start with a frequent task such as customer follow-up, invoicing, or recording a payment. Write the steps in the order they should happen.

REPEAT

Protect consistency

Use a checklist and clear handoffs so customers receive the same reliable service even when the usual person is unavailable.

IMPROVE

Review the workflow

Keep procedures current as tools, customer needs, and the business change. Documentation should help the owner improve the work, not just repeat it.

Part 4 | Compliance

Know which requirements apply.

The chapter asks owners to review requirements at local, provincial, national, and international levels. Areas to check include business structure, data privacy, employment, environmental rules, financial regulation, health and safety, insurance, intellectual property, and licences or permits. I will confirm the obligations for my actual business and location before relying on assumptions.

Part 5 | Employees

Hire carefully and help people grow.

A detailed job description clarifies the work and the skills needed. The book also emphasizes prayerful, methodical hiring and leadership that gives employees opportunities to contribute and develop.

01

Hire slowly, act promptly

Take care to assess the role and candidate, then address a poor fit without allowing the problem to harm the team or business.

02

Provide adequate training

Give new employees clear expectations, useful training, and a path to take on more responsibility.

03

Inspect more, assume less

Stay close to the work by asking questions, listening, and guiding without micromanaging.

Part 6 | Goals

Use SMART goals and keep adjusting.

Break the vision into goals that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Work backward from a longer-term outcome to shorter milestones, then work consistently, track progress, and adjust when appropriate.

SpecificDefine the result

Say exactly what I intend to accomplish.

MeasurableTrack progress

Choose an indicator that shows whether I am moving forward.

AchievableMake it realistic

Match the goal to available time, resources, and current capacity.

Relevant + time-boundSet the reason and date

Connect the goal to the vision and choose when it should be done.

Part 7 | Presentation

Prepare a clear five-minute business presentation.

Use the Personal Business Planner to explain the customer need, offer, advantage, target customer, customer plan, resources, path to profitability, and next steps. Practise to stay within five minutes and dress professionally.
