Growing My Business

This chapter turns a successful core offer into a thoughtful growth plan: choose where to grow, prepare for the resources and risks, and protect the business's financial foundation.

Chapter learning focus

Three ways to grow with purpose

01

Expand services or products

Develop new offerings for existing target customers that fit the core business and solve a related need.

02

Expand the target market

Research a new group or area, then plan how to reach more customers without letting service quality fall.

03

Compete for more market share

Understand what competitors do well and improve quality or customer value so people have a reason to switch.

Plan before expanding

Growth should follow evidence and capacity.

Before committing resources, compare customer demand with the money, people, time, and equipment available. Identify what must be true first, what could go wrong, and how to preserve the dependable IT support, cybersecurity guidance, and cloud administration at the center of the business.

Saving and cutting costs

Build a stronger financial base

Consistent savings can create flexibility for future opportunities. Cost reductions should protect the work that produces the most value for customers.

SAVE

Make it consistent

Choose a realistic amount to set aside each week and review progress regularly.

FOCUS

Keep what earns

Prioritize the services that customers value and that contribute most to sustainable income.

REDUCE

Spend deliberately

Reuse or share equipment, use suitable technology, compare suppliers, and negotiate before buying.
