Starting and Growing My Business | Chapter 4

Finding Customers

This week's lesson focuses on moving from a business idea to a clear customer, a useful message, focused sales channels, and measurable action.

6Learning parts
1Make-a-sale challenge
NextTest the message with customers

Chapter learning focus

From customer understanding to customer action

01
Learn

Put customers at the center

Build long-term relationships, listen to feedback, provide exceptional service, and make each interaction useful to the customer.

02
Learn

Describe the target customer

Be specific about demographic, organizational, geographic, psychographic, and behavioral factors instead of treating everyone as the target market.

03
Learn

Write a business message

State the business name, the service or product offered, and what makes it different from competitors in a short message that another person can understand.

04
Learn

Choose focused sales channels

Start with one physical channel and one e-commerce channel that fit the target customer and the available budget.

05
Learn

Attract the right customers

Use practical methods such as providing information, aligning with customer interests, allowing experience, building confidence, providing convenience, and partnering.

06
Learn

Measure communication

Count the customer responses that come from each communication method so busy work can be separated from activity that is producing results.
