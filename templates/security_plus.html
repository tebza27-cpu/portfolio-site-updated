CompTIA Security+ SY0-701

A practical study guide, exam-preparation plan, and curated resource list for the current CompTIA Security+ exam. Use this page alongside the supplied full training guide, printer-friendly guide, acronym list, and topic notes.

Exam Snapshot

  • Exam: CompTIA Security+ SY0-701
  • Format: Up to 90 questions, including multiple-choice and performance-based questions
  • Time limit: 90 minutes
  • Passing score: 750 on a scale of 100-900
  • Approach: Learn the concepts, practise applying them to scenarios, then close gaps with timed questions

Exam Domains

Governance, risk, compliance, third-party risk, policies, audits, awareness, and business continuity.

Study Guide From the Attached Material

Use these topics as focused revision sessions after reading the corresponding sections in the supplied training guide.

Attack Surface and Defense in Depth

Map exposure across OSI layers, perimeter controls, network zones, and application services. Know why single points of failure, undocumented dependencies, and overreliance on perimeter security create risk.

  • Layers 1-2: physical ports, wireless access, and local broadcast exposure.
  • Layer 3: addressing, spoofing, routing, and zone-to-zone communication.
  • Layers 4-7: TCP/UDP ports, protocols, applications, and services.

Gap Analysis

Practise comparing current controls against a framework or compliance requirement, scoring gaps, documenting weak controls, and recommending remediation.

  1. Define the framework or requirement.
  2. Record the current control and evidence.
  3. Rate the gap, risk, and business impact.
  4. Assign remediation and a target date.

Hardening Concepts

Review default credentials, unnecessary services, patching, secure management protocols, ACLs, port security, firewalls, logging, antimalware, and physical security.

  • Replace defaults and enforce strong authentication.
  • Disable unused interfaces, services, and insecure protocols.
  • Patch, monitor, log, and protect the device physically.

Network Access Control

Understand authentication, posture checks, dynamic VLAN assignment, quarantine, and the differences between agent-based and agentless NAC.

  • Agent-based NAC gives detailed device status and can support remediation.
  • Agentless NAC can use scans, DHCP fingerprinting, or port-based controls.
  • Noncompliant devices can be restricted or placed in a quarantine VLAN.

Secure Protocols

Compare HTTPS, SSH, SFTP, FTPS, LDAPS, IMAPS, POP3S, SRTP, SIPS, NTS, DNSSEC, and the security limitations of protocols such as FTP, Telnet, and HTTP.

Exam habit: identify the communication scenario first, then choose the protocol that protects the required traffic and authentication.

Six-Week Preparation Plan

  1. Week 1: Read the official objectives and build a baseline quiz score. Start an acronym and command reference.
  2. Week 2: Study general concepts, threats, vulnerabilities, and mitigations. Explain each control in your own words.
  3. Week 3: Study architecture, segmentation, cloud security, resiliency, and the attached attack-surface and hardening notes.
  4. Week 4: Study security operations, identity, endpoint security, vulnerability management, logging, and incident response.
  5. Week 5: Study governance, risk, compliance, third-party risk, and the attached gap-analysis and NAC notes.
  6. Week 6: Take timed practice exams, review every missed question, and repeat weak objectives until you can apply them to scenarios.

Exam-Ready Checklist

  • Read every SY0-701 objective and mark confidence by topic.
  • Know the purpose and best use of common secure protocols.
  • Practise interpreting logs, alerts, diagrams, and configuration snippets.
  • Review ports, authentication factors, cryptography terms, and attack types.
  • Practise performance-based questions without relying on memorised answers.
  • Use elimination carefully when a question asks for the best control.
  • Review missed questions by objective, not just by score.
  • Book the exam only after consistent timed practice results.
